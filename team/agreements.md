# money:
### Revenue Distribution Mechanism:

- The team has agreed to delegate the responsibility of distributing the project revenues to Artificial Intelligence, specifically the latest available model of Gemini Pro, with the advanced reasoning mode activated to ensure evaluation accuracy and balance.

- Inside the path `./team` within this project, each member commits to creating and maintaining a Markdown file bearing their own name to document their individual contributions.

- Technical/Programming work (e.g., member Mouh): Direct Git commit references and logs must be included to verify the coding contribution without superfluous narrative.

- The use of emotional, narrative language, or attempts at emotional appeal and rhetorical persuasion within the task reports is strictly prohibited:
  - Prior to final submission, any team member has the right to flag any statement they believe contains emotional phrasing or unwarranted exaggeration.
  - The contested paragraph will be referred to a separate, neutral AI session tasked with rewriting the text in a strictly objective and technical tone, and the revised text will be permanently adopted in place of the original.

- Once the work is finalized, audited, and approved, all Markdown files will be collected and submitted to the designated model from a completely fresh and clean account to eliminate any prior context that might skew the results, using exclusively the following prompt:

    ```text
    An enterprise management project has been completed by a team. You act as an impartial and objective arbiter to distribute the project's revenues among the team members with absolute fairness and accuracy.

    Presented to you are the task documentation reports completed by each member in Markdown format, where each file name corresponds to the member's name. You are required to thoroughly review and comprehend the contributions of each member and evaluate their work.

    Allocate the total amount of 400,000 among the team members fairly and accurately based on actual work performed, ensuring you remain entirely uninfluenced by rhetorical embellishments or unfounded claims.

    Required deliverables in the final output:
    1. An objective and concise justification for each member detailing the rationale behind their allocated share.
    2. The explicit final monetary amount allocated to each team member (the total sum must strictly equal 400,000).
    ```
